Having Fun on Mallorcan Roads

2025 April 10 One-minute read
Collection•Road•Mallorca•Spain•2025

In April, my friend and I spent five unforgettable days cycling through the stunning roads of Mallorca. From the coastlines to the mountains, the island offered a perfect mix of scenic views, challenging climbs, and thrilling descents. Whether riding along the crystal-clear waters or through charming villages, we discovered the true beauty of Mallorca one pedal stroke at a time. This collection captures our adventure across the island, showcasing some of the best routes that combine fun, challenge, and breathtaking landscapes.
